# Wonder Bites: content guidelines

These rules apply to every script, whether a human or `ytc draft` wrote it.
The channel is for kids aged 6–12, so the bar is higher than for a general
channel. **If in doubt, leave it out.**

## 1. Facts

- **True, checkable, current.** Every episode lists at least two reputable
  sources (NASA, NOAA, Smithsonian, National Geographic, Britannica, museums,
  aquariums, universities). A human opens every source before publishing.
- **No exaggeration.** Say "about", "up to" and "some" when that's what the
  source says. Round numbers into kid-friendly comparisons, but keep them honest.
- **Debunk, don't spread, myths.** If a popular "fact" is wrong (goldfish have
  3-second memories, bats are blind), the episode can be about *why it's wrong*.
- **One idea per Short.** A kid should be able to repeat the fact at dinner.

## 2. Language

- Sentences under 12 words where possible. Common words. Explain any new word
  the first time it appears ("hemocyanin" → "a special blue blood helper").
- Talk *with* kids, not down to them. Curious and warm, never sarcastic.
- The payoff is a quiz kids **answer out loud**. Don't say "comment below":
  comments are switched off on made-for-kids videos.
- No "smash that subscribe button". At most one gentle sign-off like
  "See you next time on Wonder Bites!"

## 3. Safety

- Nothing a child could copy and get hurt: no fire, chemicals, heights,
  holding breath, eating wild things, or "try this at home" with risk.
- No medical advice, weight or appearance talk, or anything that could scare
  kids about their own health.
- Predators can hunt, but nothing gets hurt on screen. Say "gobbles up its
  snack", not graphic detail.
- No real crimes, disasters with casualties, wars, or current news.
- No brands, products, toys or apps. No links that send kids off YouTube.
- No personal questions ("what's your name / where do you live?").

## 4. Spooky Bites (Fridays)

Spooky Bites should feel like campfire spooky: shivers, then smiles.

**Allowed:** creaky houses, fog, full moons, owls, bats, friendly ghosts,
mysterious noises, suspense, a little "ooooh".

**Never:**

- gore, blood, injury, or death of people or pets
- kidnapping, strangers taking kids, parents in danger, kids lost and alone
  with no rescue
- demons, possession, curses, or anything religious-scary
- real places or real people claimed to be "actually haunted"
- jump-scares, sudden loud sounds, screaming, or flashing images
- a scary ending. **Every story resolves in the same video with a reassuring
  or funny twist** (the "ghost" is the cat, the "monster" wants a friend).

**Creepy-but-True** episodes (anglerfish, glowing mushrooms) are framed with
*wonder*, not fear: "nature is amazing", not "nature is out to get you".

## 5. Visual and audio

- Bright, friendly visuals. No strobing: never more than 3 flashes per second
  (photosensitive viewers).
- No sudden loud SFX. The pipeline normalises loudness to -14 LUFS.
- Thumbnails and titles must match the video. No fake shocked faces, no
  clickbait the video doesn't deliver.

## 6. Being an honest automated channel

YouTube demonetises *inauthentic*, mass-produced, repetitive content. The
pipeline is a production tool, not a content cannon.

- Every script is original, researched, and read by a human before render.
- Every render is watched start to finish by a human before upload.
- Vary topics, backgrounds, characters and structure. Never re-upload the
  same video with small changes.
- Descriptions say the narration is a text-to-speech voice.
- Publish at most about one video per day.
