## Mascot: Bitsy

A cheerful yellow star with a bite taken out of one arm, because learning is
delicious. Bitsy appears on the avatar, banner and watermark, and can appear in
videos as the sprites `bitsy` and `bitsy_wink` (drawn by `ytc brand`).

Personality: endlessly curious, a bit goofy, always impressed ("WHOA!"). Bitsy
never pretends to know everything and loves saying "scientists are still
figuring that out!"

## Voice

- **Wonder Bites narrator:** Piper `kristin` voice (public-domain training
  data), slightly slowed down. Warm, bright, curious.
- **Spooky Bites storyteller:** Piper `norman` voice, slower. Campfire uncle
  energy: hushed, playful, never menacing.

## Type

- **Luckiest Guy** (Apache 2.0): headlines, captions, thumbnails. Chunky,
  playful, readable on a phone.
- **Fredoka** (OFL): labels, banner tagline, small text.

Font files and licences are in `assets/fonts/`.

## Art

- Characters: **Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D** (MIT licence, credited in every
  description). Cached in `assets/sprites/`.
- Backgrounds, mascot, music and SFX: generated by this repo's code, so the
  channel owns them outright.
